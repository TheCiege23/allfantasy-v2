# AllFantasy.AI — World Cup Beta Tester Checklist

**Version:** Beta  
**Last updated:** 2026-05-23  
**Target:** Internal beta testers / early-access users

Thank you for testing AllFantasy.AI World Cup! Please work through the steps below and note anything that felt broken, confusing, or missing. Your feedback directly shapes the launch.

---

## Before You Start

- You need an AllFantasy account. Sign up at the main login page if you don't have one.
- Test on at least **one mobile device** (phone browser) and **one desktop browser** if possible.
- Note your language preference and mode (light/dark/AF) so we can reproduce any issues.

---

## STEP 1 — Account & Setup

1. **Sign in** to your AllFantasy account.
2. Go to **Profile / Settings** and set your **language preference** (English, Español, 中文, Filipino, or Tiếng Việt).
3. Switch between **light mode, dark mode, and AF mode** if you can find the toggle.
4. Navigate to **`/brackets`**. Confirm the World Cup hub loads.
5. Navigate to **`/brackets/world-cup`**. Confirm your existing pools (or an empty state) appear.

**What to note:**
- Did the language switch take effect immediately or require a page reload?
- Is the page readable in your chosen mode?
- Did anything look broken on mobile?

---

## STEP 2 — Create a Pool

1. Click **Create Pool** (or use `/brackets/world-cup/create`).
2. Fill in a pool name and settings.
3. Submit the form.
4. Confirm you land on the **pool dashboard** with a success message.
5. Copy your **invite code** or invite link from the **Invite tab**.

**What to note:**
- Were the form fields easy to understand?
- Did the create form work on mobile?
- Was the invite link easy to find?

---

## STEP 3 — Discover & Join a Pool

1. Go to **`/brackets/world-cup/discover`**. Confirm public pools are listed (or a clear empty state).
2. Use an invite code (from a teammate or the one you just created) to join a pool at **`/brackets/world-cup/join`**.
3. Confirm you land on the **pool dashboard** after joining.
4. Try re-joining the same pool — confirm you are redirected to the dashboard instead of creating a duplicate.

**What to note:**
- Was the join/invite flow clear?
- What happened with a bad invite code — was the error friendly?

---

## STEP 4 — Make Picks

1. From the pool dashboard, open **Group Stage**.
2. Pick the **top 2 teams** from each group.
3. Select **third-place advancers** if prompted.
4. Open **Knockouts** and complete all matchup picks through to the final.
5. Confirm picks are **saved automatically** (no explicit save button needed).
6. Leave and return to the dashboard — confirm picks persist.

**What to note:**
- Did the bracket board scroll horizontally on mobile?
- Were the pick buttons easy to tap on a small screen?
- Did any save state feel uncertain?

---

## STEP 5 — Finalize Your Bracket

1. Open the **Review tab**.
2. If any picks are missing, confirm the checklist highlights them clearly.
3. Click **Finalize bracket**.
4. Confirm the success state is clear.

**What to note:**
- Was it obvious what "finalize" means and that it locks your picks?
- Was the missing-picks checklist helpful?

---

## STEP 6 — AI Features

> Some AI features require **AF Pro**. If you are on a free account, you will see a "Locked" badge and a description of what Pro unlocks. This is expected.

### 6a — Explain My Bracket
1. Open the **Review tab** after finalizing.
2. Find the **Explain My Bracket** card (look for the sparkle icon).
3. If you have Pro access, click **Generate explanation** and wait for the AI analysis.
4. If you do not have Pro, confirm the locked state shows a clear description of what you get with Pro.

**What to note:**
- Was the card discoverable?
- Did the explanation make sense for your picks?
- Was the language correct (Spanish/Chinese/Filipino/Vietnamese if you changed language)?

### 6b — AI Bracket Report / Insights
1. In the Review tab, look for AI-generated insights (Rooting Guide, Danger Zones, Path To Win, Uniqueness).
2. Confirm content appears or a clear loading/locked state is shown.

### 6c — Ask Chimmy in /ai-chat
1. Navigate to **`/ai-chat`**.
2. Ask a general fantasy/World Cup question (e.g., "Who should I root for if Brazil gets knocked out?").
3. Confirm the response arrives and is in your selected language.
4. Ask: **"What sports games are being played today?"** — confirm the app responds safely without making up fake games.
5. If you hit a daily cap, confirm the message is polite and explains the limit.

**What to note:**
- Did Chimmy respond in the right language?
- Did the schedule guardrail work (no hallucinated game times)?
- Was the daily cap message clear?

### 6d — Commissioner Brain (Commissioners only)
1. If you are the pool commissioner, go to the **Commissioner tab**.
2. Find the **Bracket Brain** section.
3. Try generating hype, standings summary, or a recap message.
4. Confirm the output appears and can be sent to the pool chat.

**What to note:**
- Was the Commissioner Brain panel visible only to you (not to regular members)?
- Did the output feel appropriate?

---

## STEP 7 — Invite & Share

1. From the **Invite tab**, copy the invite link.
2. Paste it in a separate browser (or send to someone) — confirm it lands on the join page with pool details.
3. Copy the bracket **share text** from the Review tab (if available).
4. From the **Commissioner view**, confirm the member completion checklist shows who has and hasn't finalized.

**What to note:**
- Was the invite link working correctly?
- Did the share copy feel shareable?
- Was the commissioner completion view accurate?

---

## STEP 8 — Language & Mode Final Check

1. Switch language mid-session (e.g., from English to Spanish).
2. Confirm pool dashboard labels, tab names, and AI responses update.
3. Switch between light/dark/AF mode.
4. Confirm the bracket board, cards, and buttons are readable in all modes.

**What to note:**
- Any tab label that stayed in English after switching?
- Any unreadable text in dark mode or AF mode?
- Any untranslated strings in Spanish/Chinese/Filipino/Vietnamese?
